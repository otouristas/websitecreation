**Link building** is the practice of earning hyperlinks from other websites to yours. Search engines treat quality links as votes of trust. Done poorly, it risks penalties; done well, it compounds [off-page SEO](/en/blog/off-page-seo).

## White-hat link building tactics

1. Create linkable assets (original data, tools, definitive guides)
2. Digital PR and journalist outreach
3. Relevant guest contributions (editorial, not spam farms)
4. Broken-link and resource-page opportunities
5. Partnerships and industry associations

Avoid PBNs, mass blog comment spam, and irrelevant paid links.
