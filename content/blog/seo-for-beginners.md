## What SEO means for beginners

[SEO](/en/glossary/seo) (search engine optimization) is the set of changes that help a site appear in unpaid results. Beginners move forward with a short list: one page per customer question, a title a person can understand, a site Google can crawl, and a look at which queries already earn impressions.

A [keyword](/en/glossary/keywords) is the phrase someone types. [Search intent](/en/glossary/search-intent) is why they typed it: to learn, to compare, or to buy. Match the page to that reason. A pricing page should not chase a definition.

## How Google ranks a page

Three stages, in this order:

1. **[Crawling](/en/glossary/crawling).** Bots discover URLs by following links and reading the sitemap.
2. **[Indexing](/en/glossary/indexing).** Pages that are allowed and useful are stored so they can be retrieved.
3. **Ranking.** For each search, Google orders results by how relevant, trustworthy, and usable they are.

![Diagram of three SEO stages: crawling discovers the URL, indexing stores the page, and ranking orders it for a search](/images/blog/seo-for-beginners-flow.svg)

If a page is never crawled, copy and links cannot help it. Do [technical SEO](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide) before a pile of new posts. Confirm the important URLs in [Google Search Console](/en/glossary/google-search-console) first.

## The four pillars of SEO

### Keywords

People search the words they use. Collect phrases from sales calls, autocomplete, and Search Console, then give each money page one primary phrase. The method is in the [keyword research guide](/en/blog/keyword-research-guide). A free starting point is the [keyword research tool](/en/tools/keyword-research).

### On-page SEO

[On-page SEO](/en/glossary/on-page-seo-term) is what you control on the URL itself:

- **[Title tag](/en/glossary/title-tag):** the headline in the result, with the phrase near the front.
- **[Meta description](/en/glossary/meta-description):** the lines under the title that earn the click.
- **One H1 and logical H2s,** short paragraphs, and links to the next useful page.
- **An answer** that is clearer than the pages already ranking, not merely longer.

Checklist: [on-page SEO](/en/blog/on-page-seo).

### Technical SEO

Speed, mobile layout, HTTPS, clean URLs, an XML sitemap, and [structured data](/en/glossary/structured-data) when the page type is real (FAQ, local business, product). Start with the [technical SEO guide](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide) or a [technical SEO audit](/en/services/seo-audits). A [free SEO audit](/en/tools/free-seo-audit) spots blocked pages and missing titles.

### Authority

A [backlink](/en/glossary/backlink) is a link from another site. One relevant editorial mention is worth more than a pile of directory spam. Read [link building](/en/blog/link-building-guide) and [off-page SEO](/en/blog/off-page-seo) once the site is worth linking to.

## Local search and AI answers

If customers visit you or search with a city, Google often shows a map above the usual results. That [local pack](/en/glossary/local-pack) depends on a complete [Google Business Profile](/en/glossary/google-business-profile): category, hours, photos, and replies to reviews. See [local SEO](/en/blog/local-seo-guide) and [local SEO services](/en/services/local-seo).

People also ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google [AI Overviews](/en/glossary/ai-overview). Those answers still need a page that states the fact, names the business, and can be cited. Put the definition in the opening and keep the FAQ on the page. The wider guide is the [AI search playbook](/en/blog/geo-aeo-global-seo-playbook), with the service at [AI visibility](/en/services/ai-visibility).

## A 30-day SEO plan for beginners

Do the days in order. Publishing ahead of crawl fixes creates pages Google has not been invited to read.

| Days | Job | Done when |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1–2 | Add the site to Google Search Console and GA4. Submit the sitemap. | You can open the Indexing and Performance reports. |
| 3–7 | Fix HTTPS, accidental noindex, and the worst mobile failures on the homepage and contact page. | Those URLs are eligible to be indexed. |
| 8–14 | Rewrite the title, H1, and opening on your top five service or product pages. One primary phrase each. | Each page says who it is for in the first screen. |
| 15–18 | Complete Google Business Profile if you serve a place: category, hours, photos, services. | The profile matches the site name, address, and phone. |
| 19–24 | Publish two answers customers already ask. Link each from the related service page. | Both URLs are in the sitemap and linked internally. |
| 25–27 | Add [internal links](/en/glossary/internal-linking) from stronger pages to the pages you want found. | No important page is reachable only from the menu. |
| 28–30 | Read Search Console. Note queries with impressions and a weak click-through rate. Queue title rewrites. | You have a written list for month two. |

Need help with the writing? See the [SEO content guide](/en/blog/seo-content-guide) and [content creation](/en/services/content-creation). A new site should be crawlable from day one: [SEO web design](/en/services/seo-web-design).

## Beginner mistakes to skip

1. **Publishing dozens of posts before the homepage and service pages can be indexed.** Volume does not repair a blocked site.
2. **Repeating one phrase in every sentence.** Write the answer. Use the phrase in the title and the headings.
3. **Ignoring the local profile** when customers search with a city or "near me".
4. **Buying a package that promises a first-place ranking.** Those offers usually mean spam links.
5. **Watching only a rank tracker.** Also watch [impressions](/en/glossary/impressions), [clicks](/en/glossary/clicks), and whether anyone calls or fills the form.
6. **Sending ads to a slow, vague page.** You pay for the visit either way.
7. **Stopping in week three.** The 30-day plan builds the base. It is not the whole project.

## What to check after 90 days

A fair 90-day check is simple: money pages indexed, rewritten titles visible in results, impressions on the queries you chose, and a few organic enquiries you can trace. Keep hours, photos, and review replies current if you rely on the map.

Impressions up and clicks flat means rewrite titles and descriptions. Clicks up and enquiries flat means fix the offer, the proof, and the form. Ads can bring visits this week while you pay; SEO remains when a campaign pauses. Many businesses run both. See [SEO vs Google Ads](/en/blog/seo-vs-google-ads).
