**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** is the practice of improving a website so it ranks higher in unpaid Google results — and increasingly gets cited in AI answers like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. This pillar guide explains how SEO works, the four types, beginners’ first steps, realistic costs and best practices you can apply today.

For a shorter plain-English path, see [SEO for beginners](/en/blog/seo-for-beginners). For commercial programs, start with [SEO audit services](/en/services/seo-audits) or [Get Started](/en/get-started).

## How Search Engine Optimization Works

1. **Crawling** — bots discover URLs by following links and sitemaps.
2. **Indexing** — eligible pages enter Google’s searchable index.
3. **Ranking** — for each query, Google orders results by relevance, quality and trust.

SEO helps at all three stages. Related terms people use interchangeably include **website SEO**, **SEO marketing** and **SEO strategy** — they all sit under this same process. If pages never get crawled or indexed, content and links cannot help. That is why [technical SEO](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide) comes before publishing more blogs.

## The 4 Types of SEO

### 1. Technical SEO

Crawlability, speed, mobile usability, HTTPS, canonicals, structured data and clean architecture. Start with a [technical SEO guide](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide) or an [SEO audit](/en/services/seo-audits).

### 2. On-page SEO

Titles, headings, content, internal links and media on each URL. Deep dive: [on-page SEO](/en/blog/on-page-seo).

### 3. Off-page SEO (including link building)

Authority signals earned outside your site — mainly backlinks. See [off-page SEO](/en/blog/off-page-seo) and [link building](/en/blog/link-building-guide).

### 4. Local SEO

Map pack and “near me” visibility via Google Business Profile, citations and location pages. Guide: [local SEO](/en/blog/local-seo-guide) · Service: [local SEO services](/en/services/local-seo).

**Content SEO** and **AI visibility (GEO/AEO)** cut across these pillars — see [SEO content](/en/blog/seo-content-guide) and [AI SEO services](/en/services/ai-visibility).

## SEO Best Practices Checklist

- One primary intent per money page
- Clear title + meta that earns the click
- Fast, mobile-first templates
- Helpful content that answers PAA-style questions
- Internal links from strong pages to new ones
- Relevant backlinks over raw volume
- Measure in Search Console weekly

## Google Ranking Factors (Plain View)

Google does not publish a full ranking formula. In practice, pages that match intent, load well, demonstrate experience/expertise and earn trusted links tend to win. **Organic traffic** is the visit stream from those unpaid results — grow it with better coverage, CTR and authority ([how to increase organic traffic](/en/blog/how-to-increase-organic-traffic)).

AI Overviews and ChatGPT-style answers reward the same clarity: definitions early, FAQs, original proof and citable structure. SEO in 2026 is not “rank blue links only” — it is also earning mentions when assistants recommend businesses.

## Common SEO Mistakes

1. **Publishing before fixing indexation** — blogs nobody can find.
2. **Keyword stuffing** — repeats that read like a bot wrote them.
3. **Ignoring CTR** — rankings without clicks waste impressions.
4. **Buying cheap links** — spam that risks penalties and wastes budget.
5. **Expecting overnight #1** — SEO compounds; Ads are for instant demand.
6. **One thin page for every city** — duplicate location content that never ranks.
7. **Skipping measurement** — no Search Console review means no learning loop.

## A Practical 90-Day Starting Sequence

Month one is foundations: Search Console, indexation, mobile speed and titles on your highest-intent service pages. Month two expands coverage — FAQs, location or service detail and internal links from strong URLs. Month three is measurement: improve pages stuck on positions 8–20, trim wasted Ads keywords and decide whether a retainer for content or [local SEO](/en/services/local-seo) is the next lever.

This sequence beats random blogging because every week maps to a commercial outcome: more qualified impressions, better CTR or more calls. If you sell nationally, swap local map-pack work for category pages and authority. If you sell on islands or in one city, Google Business Profile and reviews usually move the needle faster than a long blog archive.

Teams that skip the sequence often publish for vanity — traffic that never converts — or buy links before the site can absorb them. Fix the pipe first, then pour content and authority through it.

## FAQ

### What is SEO marketing?

SEO marketing is using organic search as an acquisition channel — content, technical work and authority — instead of (or alongside) paid ads.

### SEO vs SEM?

SEO is organic. SEM usually means paid search. Most growth plans use both — see [SEO vs Google Ads](/en/blog/seo-vs-google-ads).

### Is SEO still worth it in 2026?

Yes. Organic search still drives high-intent traffic, and AI answers cite clear, trusted pages. Businesses that invest consistently build an asset; those that only rent Ads pay forever.

### Where should I start?
