**On-page SEO** is everything you control on a URL to match search intent: title tags, headings, body copy, media, and internal links. It is the highest-control lever after [technical SEO](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide).

## On-page SEO checklist

1. One primary keyword / intent per page
2. Unique title (keyword near the front) + meta description that earns clicks
3. Single H1; logical H2/H3 structure
4. Answer the query in the first screenful
5. FAQs for PAA / AI Overviews
6. Internal links to related services and guides
7. Descriptive image alt text; compress media
8. Schema when it matches visible content

## Common mistakes

- Keyword stuffing
- Thin pages that repeat the same service copy city after city
- Ignoring CTR (good rankings with weak titles)
- No internal links from strong pages
