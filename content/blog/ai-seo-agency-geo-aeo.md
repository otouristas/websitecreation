## SEO vs GEO vs AEO (plain English)

These three labels overlap, but they are not interchangeable. Treat them as layers on one growth system.

| Term | Primary goal | Where it shows up | Core assets |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SEO** | Rank and earn clicks | Google organic, local pack | Technical health, content, links |
| **AEO** | Win concise answers | Featured snippets, AI Overviews, voice | FAQ blocks, schema, answer-first copy |
| **GEO** | Be selected and cited | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini answers | Entities, proof pages, citation sampling |

You still need technical SEO. GEO and AEO add entity clarity, FAQ schema, citation-worthy pages, and monitoring of AI mentions. For a deeper side-by-side, read [GEO vs SEO vs AEO](/en/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo) and the [AI LLM visibility pillar](/en/blog/pillar-ai-llm-visibility).

### Why agencies rebranded as “AI SEO”

Search vendors and agencies rushed to rename retainers. The useful distinction is operational: classic SEO optimizes for crawl, rank, and click; AI-era work also optimizes for **selection** - whether a model trusts your page enough to quote it. That requires clearer entities, stronger evidence, and measurement beyond keyword rank alone.

## What generative engine optimization agency work includes

A serious program is not a plugin install or a one-off “AI rewrite.” It is a repeatable operating system.

### 1. Entity cleanup

Models and answer engines prefer consistent facts. Align brand name, NAP, services, destinations, and language variants across the site, Google Business Profile, and major profiles. Ambiguous “we” copy and conflicting addresses kill citation confidence.

### 2. FAQ + schema for AEO

Answer engines love clear question-and-answer blocks with matching FAQPage markup. Put commercial questions on money pages - pricing, inclusions, pickup points, cancellation - not only on a buried FAQ archive. Pair this with strong [on-page SEO](/en/blog/on-page-seo) so humans and parsers see the same answer.

### 3. Citation-ready pages

Publish unique stats, process steps, policies, and proof (portfolio, reviews, named locations) that models can quote. Generic brochure pages lose to OTAs and directories. Original tables, ferry distances, fleet rules, and room policies win specific prompts.

### 4. Internal linking and topical hubs

Money pages need topical authority from hubs, guides, and case studies. Orphan service URLs rarely become the cited source. Use the same discipline as classic clusters: one primary intent per URL, supporting articles that pass context.

### 5. Measurement that stays honest

Track rankings **and** branded AI mentions over time. Sample a fixed prompt set monthly; log model, date, and whether your brand was mentioned, linked, or omitted. Treat outputs as directional brand sampling - not a fake “AI rank #3.” See [LLM citations and brand visibility](/en/blog/llm-citations-brand-visibility).

## Mistakes checklist - avoid these in 2026

- Treating GEO as a replacement for crawlability, speed, and relevance
- Stuffing keywords into AI-generated blogs with no unique proof
- Adding FAQ schema that does not match visible on-page answers
- Chasing every model update instead of a fixed monthly prompt sample
- Ignoring local signals (GBP, reviews, NAP) while optimizing only for chatbots
- Claiming guaranteed ChatGPT placement in sales decks
- Measuring success only by impressions, never by leads or bookings

Use this as a monthly review checklist with your agency or in-house team.

## FAQ

### What is an AI SEO agency?

An AI SEO agency combines classic SEO with GEO and AEO so your brand ranks in Google and gets cited in AI answers. The deliverables look like entity cleanup, FAQ/schema, citation-ready pages, internal linking, and dual measurement (GSC + AI mention sampling).

### Is generative engine optimization different from content marketing?

Yes. Content marketing can raise awareness; GEO specifically designs pages and entities so generative engines can retrieve, trust, and attribute your brand. Volume without evidence usually fails citation tests.

### How fast can we see AI citations?

Entity and FAQ fixes can influence answer surfaces within weeks for some queries; competitive tourism prompts often need 2–4 months of consistent publishing and sampling. Rank and citation timelines are not identical.

### Do we still need backlinks?

Authority still helps. GEO does not cancel [link building](/en/blog/link-building-guide); it reduces reliance on links alone when your page is the clearest factual source for a niche prompt.

### Should small businesses start with GEO or SEO?

Start with technical SEO and local foundations, add AEO (FAQ + schema) on commercial pages, then expand GEO citation programs. That sequence is spelled out in [GEO vs SEO vs AEO](/en/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo).
