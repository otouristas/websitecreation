## SEO vs GEO vs AEO (plain English)

| Term | Goal | Where it shows up |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SEO** | Rank in organic results | Google blue links, local pack |
| **GEO** | Be selected & cited by generative engines | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini answers |

You still need technical SEO. GEO/AEO add entity clarity, FAQ schema, citation-worthy pages and monitoring of AI mentions.

## What generative engine optimization agency work includes

1. **Entity cleanup** — consistent NAP, brand, services and destinations across the site and profiles.
2. **FAQ + schema** — answer engines love clear Q&A with FAQPage markup.
3. **Citation-ready pages** — unique stats, process steps, and proof (portfolio, reviews) that models can quote.
4. **Internal linking** — money pages get topical authority from hubs and case studies.
5. **Measurement** — track rankings *and* branded AI mentions over time.
