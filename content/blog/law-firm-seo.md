When someone needs a lawyer, they search Google: "divorce attorney near me", "employment lawyer", "car accident lawyer". **Law firm SEO** is one of the most competitive - and most profitable - areas of digital marketing: a single case can pay for a whole year of investment.

## Why SEO Fits Law Firms

- The client has an **immediate, serious need** - they aren't browsing.
- The search is **local**: your office location and reach define the targeting.
- **Trust** matters more than anywhere: a polished site with genuinely useful content builds confidence before the first consultation.

## The Strategy in 5 Steps

### 1. A page per practice area

One "Services" page won't rank. You need separate, thorough pages: family law, employment, criminal, real estate, wills and estates. Each answers the questions clients actually ask.

### 2. Local presence

Google Business Profile with the right category, photos, and consistent review management. [Local SEO](/en/services/local-seo) captures "attorney near me" - almost always searched on mobile with intent to call.

### 3. Content that answers questions

"What am I entitled to after termination?", "how much does a divorce cost?", "what do I do after an accident?". Content that answers these responsibly brings tomorrow's clients - and appears in AI answers (see [GEO/AEO](/en/blog/geo-aeo-global-seo-playbook)).

### 4. Technical excellence and speed

Competition on legal keywords is high; technical issues that are forgiven elsewhere cost rankings here. Fast site, correct schema (LegalService, FAQPage), clean structure.

### 5. Compliance

Legal advertising has rules. SEO is the most compatible channel: you're not "advertising" - you're answering, with evidence, those already searching for you.

## FAQ

### How long until I see cases from Google?

In smaller markets, first calls often come within 2-3 months. In competitive metros for areas like divorce, expect 4-6 months of systematic work.

### Do I need a new website?

If your site is 5+ years old, slow, or not mobile-friendly, yes - a modern [website](/en/services/website-creation) is a prerequisite. See our [solutions for lawyers](/en/solutions/lawyers).

### Can I target more than one city?

Yes, with separate local pages per target city and careful structure so each page has real value - not copy-paste with a swapped city name.
