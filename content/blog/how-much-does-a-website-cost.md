## What Determines the Price

### Design: template or custom?

A template costs less but looks like thousands of other sites. Custom design costs more and builds a brand. The sweet spot: custom design on a proven technical foundation.

### Features

Forms, bookings, multilingual support, CRM integration, member areas - each feature adds development time. List what you truly need on day one versus later.

### SEO built in, or bolted on?

The biggest hidden cost: a beautiful site without SEO foundations (speed, structure, schema, clean URLs) will need expensive fixes later. Always ask for [SEO-ready development](/en/services/seo-web-design) from the start.

### Content

Copy and photos are often not included. If you don't have them ready, budget for [content creation](/en/services/content-creation).

## Recurring Costs You Should Know

- **Domain**: €10-20/year
- **Hosting**: €5-30/month for a standard site, more for a busy e-shop
- **SSL**: usually free with hosting
- **Maintenance**: updates, backups, security - from €199/month or hourly

## FAQ

### Why do quotes vary so much?

Because you're not comparing the same thing: template vs custom, with or without SEO, with or without content and maintenance. Ask for an itemized quote.
