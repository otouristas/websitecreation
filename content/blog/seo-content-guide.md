**SEO content** is content engineered to satisfy a search intent and earn organic visibility — service pages, location pages, guides, and FAQs — without sounding like a keyword dump.

## How to write SEO content (process)

1. Confirm demand with [keyword research](/en/blog/keyword-research-guide)
2. Study top results’ format (guide vs list vs service page)
3. Outline answer-first + supporting sections + FAQs
4. Add unique proof (data, examples, local detail)
5. Internally link to [services](/en/services) and related guides
6. Add schema-ready Q&A when FAQs are visible
