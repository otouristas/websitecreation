Every booking that comes through Booking.com or Expedia costs you 15-25% in commission. **Hotel SEO** is the most cost-effective way to turn those bookings back to your own site: a guest who finds you organically on Google books directly, with no middleman.

## Why SEO Is Critical for Hotels

- 75%+ of travellers research on Google before booking - even if they end up on an OTA.
- "Hotel + destination" searches carry huge seasonal value: whoever ranks in February fills up in August.
- AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) now recommend properties, pulling from well-structured websites. See our [GEO and AEO guide](/en/blog/geo-aeo-global-seo-playbook).

## The 6 Steps of Hotel SEO

### 1. A fast, mobile-first website

Bookings happen on mobile. If your site takes over 3 seconds, you lose guests before they see the rooms. Start with proper [hotel website design](/en/blog/hotel-website-design) or [speed optimization](/en/services/speed-optimization).

### 2. A page per room type and experience

One "Rooms" page isn't enough. Separate pages for suites, family rooms, sea-view rooms, breakfast, spa - each targets a different search and fills a different segment.

### 3. Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Your Maps profile drives "hotel near..." bookings. Complete profile, fresh photos, review responses. Details in [local SEO for hotels](/en/solutions/hotels/local-seo).

### 4. Destination content

Travellers search "things to do in..." before they search for a hotel. Guides about your area bring visitors early in the decision journey - and make your site the natural place to book.

### 5. Multilingual SEO with correct hreflang

The local language for domestic guests, English (and German or French where relevant) for international. Broken hreflang is one of the most common technical issues on hotel sites.

### 6. Schema markup for rich results

Hotel, Offer, and FAQ structured data help Google show prices, availability, and ratings directly - and help AI models recommend you correctly.

## FAQ

### Can I do SEO while working with Booking.com?

Yes - and you should. OTAs are a channel, not a strategy. The goal is to grow the share of direct bookings every year.

### How long until I see results?

Start in autumn or winter and your site can climb before the spring booking season. Local searches move in 2-3 months.

### Do I need a new website or just SEO?

If your site is slow, not mobile-friendly, or lacks a booking system, a [new hotel website](/en/blog/hotel-website-design) delivers far more than SEO on a broken base.
