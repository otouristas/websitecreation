Looking for **SEO tools**? In 2026 the SERP is a mix of free hubs and annual roundups. This list is practical: what to use first, what to pay for later, and where our free tools fit.

## Start free (every team)

1. **Google Search Console** — real queries and pages (non-negotiable).
2. **[Free SEO audit / health score](/en/tools/free-seo-audit)** — prioritized technical and on-page checks.
3. **[Keyword research tool](/en/tools/keyword-research)** — clusters and intent mapping.
4. **[Free SEO tools hub](/en/tools/free-seo-tools)** — meta, schema, CWV, robots, hreflang helpers.
5. **All tools index** — [/en/tools](/en/tools)

## When paid suites help

Ahrefs, Semrush, and similar platforms win on large keyword indexes, backlink graphs, and competitive research. Buy them when you publish weekly and need depth — not on day one of a brochure site.

## FAQ

### Do I need Ahrefs or Semrush?

Only when free data is no longer enough for decisions. Many SMBs grow for months on GSC + free checkers.

### Best tools for local SEO?

GBP insights, citation consistency checkers, and review workflows beat another keyword database.
