**Off-page SEO** covers ranking signals earned *outside* your website — primarily backlinks, brand mentions and reputation. It works best when [on-page SEO](/en/blog/on-page-seo) and [technical SEO](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide) are already solid. In 2026, Google and AI systems still treat relevant editorial links as trust votes, but they punish industrial spam harder than ever. This guide explains what counts, what to avoid, how much to budget, and how off-page compounds into [organic traffic](/en/blog/how-to-increase-organic-traffic).

## What Counts as Off-Page SEO in 2026

### Editorial backlinks

The core of off-page work. A link from a trusted, topically related site to a page that deserves it remains the strongest off-site signal. Focus on relevance and editorial context, not raw Domain Rating. Deep dive: [link building guide](/en/blog/link-building-guide).

### Digital PR and linkable assets

Original data, tools, maps, industry surveys and definitive guides attract coverage naturally. For Greek tourism and local businesses, destination guides, seasonal reports and unique photography packages often outperform generic guest posts.

### Brand mentions (linked and unlinked)

Mentions help discovery and entity recognition even when unlinked. Digital PR, partnerships and consistent NAP (name, address, phone) strengthen the brand graph that search and AI systems use.

### Local reputation and citations

For local intent, reviews, Google Business Profile activity and clean citations matter as much as classic blog links. See the [local SEO guide](/en/blog/local-seo-guide) and [Google Business Profile masterclass](/en/blog/google-business-profile-masterclass).

### Community and industry presence

Association memberships, event sponsorships, supplier pages and chamber listings can yield relevant links when they are real relationships — not mass directory spam.

## What Does *Not* Work Anymore

- Mass directory spam and automated profile links
- Private blog networks (PBNs) and link farms
- Irrelevant guest posts at scale on spun content sites
- Exact-match anchor dumping across dozens of sites
- Buying “100 DA50 links” packages with no topical fit

One editorial link from a trusted industry publisher usually beats fifty junk listings. If a tactic would embarrass you in a client report, skip it.

## Off-Page Checklist (Do This in Order)

1. **Confirm on-page readiness** — the target URL answers the query and converts.
2. **Fix internal linking** so equity can flow — [internal linking audit](/en/blog/internal-linking-audit-checklist).
3. **Audit existing backlinks** — remove or disavow only clear spam when needed.
4. **Define linkable assets** — what would a journalist or partner actually want?
5. **Build a niche prospect list** — relevance over vanity metrics.
6. **Outreach with value** — data, quotes, corrections, resource updates.
7. **Track placements and anchors** — natural mix; brand and naked URLs dominate.
8. **Maintain local reputation** — review replies, photo freshness, accurate citations.

## Seven Off-Page Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Buying links before fixing the site** — you pay to promote a weak page.
2. **Chasing Domain Authority alone** — a high-DA unrelated site helps less than a mid-tier niche publisher.
3. **Identical anchors everywhere** — looks manipulative; diversify.
4. **Ignoring unlinked mentions** — easy wins when partners already talk about you.
5. **Neglecting reviews for local queries** — off-page is not only blogs.
6. **No reporting** — if you cannot show URLs, anchors and relevance, you are guessing.
7. **Expecting overnight rank jumps** — authority compounds; patience plus consistency wins.

## How to Prioritize Off-Page Work by Business Type

**Local service businesses** (clinics, gyms, lawyers): prioritize Google Business Profile, review velocity, citation consistency and a handful of relevant local/editorial links. Heavy national PR is usually overkill until you dominate the city pack. Start with [local SEO services](/en/services/local-seo).

**E-commerce and national brands:** you need topical authority — category guides, digital PR and links that support money categories. Fix thin product copy first; then earn links to category hubs, not only the homepage.

**Hotels and tourism:** destination publishers, travel blogs, tourism boards and experience partners matter more than generic tech blogs. Combine off-page with room-page architecture from the [hotel SEO guide](/en/blog/seo-for-hotels-guide).

**B2B / agencies:** original research, tools and thought-leadership assets outperform directory submissions. One cited study can earn more qualified links than months of low-quality guest posts.

Whatever the niche, report monthly: new referring domains, relevance notes, target URLs supported and any toxic patterns cleaned. If a vendor only sends a Domain Rating chart with no URLs, you are not buying off-page SEO — you are buying a vanity metric. ## FAQ

### What is off-page SEO?

Signals earned outside your site — mainly backlinks, mentions and reputation — that support pages which already deserve to rank.

### How do I improve it?

Earn relevant editorial links, clean toxic spam, strengthen brand mentions and keep local reputation healthy. Pair with strong [on-page SEO](/en/blog/on-page-seo).

### Off-page vs link building?

Link building is the primary tactic inside off-page SEO. Off-page also covers PR, mentions, reviews and citations.

### How much should I spend?

White-hat programs often run €599–€999+/mo inside retainers, or scoped campaigns from ~€400/mo. See [how much SEO costs](/en/blog/how-much-does-seo-cost).

### Will bad links tank my site?

They can. Prefer quality, relevance and gradual growth. Avoid networks and irrelevant paid placements.
