**Off-page SEO** covers ranking signals earned *outside* your website — primarily backlinks, brand mentions, and reputation. It works best when [on-page SEO](/en/blog/on-page-seo) and [technical SEO](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide) are already solid.

## What counts as off-page SEO?

- **Link building** — editorial backlinks from relevant sites ([guide](/en/blog/link-building-guide) · [services](/en/services/link-building))
- **Digital PR** — newsworthy assets that attract coverage
- **Brand mentions** — even unlinked mentions can help discovery
- **Local reputation** — reviews and citations for [local SEO](/en/blog/local-seo-guide)

## What does *not* work in 2026

- Mass directory spam
- Paid link networks
- Irrelevant guest posts at scale

Quality and topical relevance beat volume. One link from a trusted industry site outperforms dozens of junk listings.

## How off-page ties to organic traffic

Authority helps competitive queries move. Pair link earning with content that deserves links — see [SEO content](/en/blog/seo-content-guide) and [how to increase organic traffic](/en/blog/how-to-increase-organic-traffic).
