## Quick definitions

- **SEO** - rank and earn clicks in traditional search results (organic listings, local pack where relevant).
- **GEO** - Generative Engine Optimization: become a trusted source generative models cite in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and similar surfaces.
- **AEO** - Answer Engine Optimization: win concise answers (featured snippets, AI Overviews, voice assistants).

None of these replaces conversion design. A #1 ranking or a perfect citation that lands on a broken booking form still wastes demand. Pair visibility work with clear CTAs and the same discipline you use in [what is SEO](/en/blog/what-is-seo) fundamentals.

Read this table as roles on one team, not competing products. AEO pages help GEO; GEO proof strengthens SEO trust; SEO crawl health unlocks both.

### Where people get confused

Vendors sometimes sell “GEO packages” that are only AI-written blogs, or “AEO audits” that are only schema plugs. Useful work always changes what is visible on the page, how entities are named, and how you measure selection - not just what a CMS plugin claims.

## Recommended sequence for leads

Rushing straight to ChatGPT sampling while the site cannot crawl or convert is the most expensive mistake we see.

### Phase 1 - SEO foundations

1. Fix crawl, indexation, speed, titles, and money-page relevance.
2. Map one primary intent per URL; kill thin duplicates.
3. Stabilize local signals if you sell locally (GBP, NAP, reviews).

Use [technical SEO](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide) and [local SEO](/en/blog/local-seo-guide) checklists before you expand AI surfaces.

### Phase 2 - AEO on commercial pages

1. Add FAQ blocks that mirror real queries from Search Console.
2. Answer in 40–60 words immediately under the question heading.
3. Implement FAQPage (and relevant LocalBusiness / Product / Lodging) schema that matches visible text.
4. Prefer tables and step lists for comparison and how-to queries.

### Phase 3 - GEO citation programs

1. Publish citation-worthy case studies, policies, and entity-clear service pages.
2. Align brand facts across site and profiles.
3. Build a fixed prompt library (20–50 prompts) and sample monthly.
4. Close gaps where competitors are mentioned and you are not.

Deeper measurement guidance: [LLM citations and brand visibility](/en/blog/llm-citations-brand-visibility) and the [AI LLM visibility pillar](/en/blog/pillar-ai-llm-visibility).

### Phase 4 - Dual reporting

Measure with GSC clicks **and** AI mention checks. Report trends over quarters. Do not promise guaranteed model placement.

## Industry examples (tourism and local)

**Hotels:** Generic “best hotel [island]” prompts favor OTAs. Specific prompts about ferry distance, family rooms, or airport transfers favor official sites with explicit policies - classic AEO structure plus GEO proof.

**Rent-a-car:** Deposit, insurance, transmission, and pickup rules stated in plain language get cited; image-only fleets do not.

**Local services:** Map pack (SEO/local) still drives calls; AI Overviews increasingly summarize “best [service] near me.” FAQ + GBP consistency covers both.

For multi-market patterns, see the [GEO/AEO global playbook](/en/blog/geo-aeo-global-seo-playbook).

### What “winning” looks like on each layer

On SEO, winning means stable rankings and clicks on money queries with healthy CTR. On AEO, winning means your concise answer is selected - snippet, Overview bullet, or voice response - often before a click happens. On GEO, winning means the model names your brand (ideally with a link) when a buyer asks for a shortlist. Those outcomes can diverge: you may rank well and still be absent from chat answers, or be cited while organic CTR drops because Overviews absorb attention. Plan for both click and non-click visibility.

## Mistakes checklist

- Buying “GEO” without fixing crawl errors and thin money pages
- Adding schema that invents FAQs not shown on the page
- Treating every model update as a full strategy reset
- Ignoring local SEO because “AI will replace Maps”
- Measuring only screenshots from one ChatGPT session
- Publishing 50 AI articles instead of 5 evidence-rich pages
- Skipping conversion tracking while chasing mentions

Run this checklist in monthly ops meetings before you expand scope.

## FAQ

### Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. Without crawlable, fast, authoritative pages, generative engines have little trustworthy material to cite. GEO adds entity and citation optimization on top.

### What does AEO stand for?

Answer Engine Optimization - structuring content and schema so answer engines can select your brand for concise answers, including AI Overviews and voice.

### Which should a small business start with?

Technical SEO + local SEO first, then FAQ/schema AEO on commercial pages, then GEO citation programs once foundations are solid.

### Do SEO tools measure GEO?

Most classic suites still focus on rank and backlinks. Treat AI mention sampling as a separate workflow (prompt library + logs), then connect it to GSC and CRM outcomes.

### Can agencies guarantee ChatGPT citations?

No credible agency can. They can improve odds with clearer entities, better evidence, and consistent measurement - then show trend lines, not guarantees.
