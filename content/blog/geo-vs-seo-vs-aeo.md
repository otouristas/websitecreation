## Quick definitions

- **SEO** — rank and earn clicks in traditional search results.
- **GEO** — Generative Engine Optimization: become a trusted source generative models cite.
- **AEO** — Answer Engine Optimization: win concise answers (snippets, AI Overviews, voice).

## Comparison table

| Capability | SEO | AEO | GEO |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Classic rankings | Primary | Support | Support |
| Featured snippets / AI Overviews | Helpful | Primary | Helpful |
| ChatGPT / Perplexity citations | Indirect | Helpful | Primary |
| Local map pack | Local SEO | Limited | Limited |
| Needs strong site fundamentals | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Recommended sequence for leads

1. Fix crawl, speed, titles and money-page relevance (**SEO**).
2. Add FAQ blocks + FAQPage schema on commercial pages (**AEO**).
3. Publish citation-worthy case studies and entity-clear service pages (**GEO**).
4. Measure with GSC clicks **and** AI mention checks.
