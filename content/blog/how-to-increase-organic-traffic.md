**Organic traffic** is visits from unpaid search (and, increasingly, AI systems that cite your pages). Growing it is the outcome of good [SEO](/en/blog/what-is-seo) — not a single trick.

## Five levers that move organic traffic

1. **Coverage** — publish pages for real demand ([keyword research](/en/blog/keyword-research-guide))
2. **CTR** — titles and metas that earn the click at your current position
3. **Indexation & tech** — fix blockers ([technical SEO](/en/blog/technical-seo-guide), [free audit](/en/tools/free-seo-audit))
4. **On-page quality** — clearer answers ([on-page SEO](/en/blog/on-page-seo), [SEO content](/en/blog/seo-content-guide))
5. **Authority** — relevant links ([link building](/en/blog/link-building-guide))

## Measure the right way

Use Search Console: impressions → clicks → average position by query and page. Compare year-over-year for seasonal businesses. Glossary: [Organic Traffic](/en/glossary?term=organic-traffic).

## Timeline

Expect compounding gains over months. Commercial long-tails move faster than head terms like “SEO.” Local and service pages often show traction first — see [local SEO services](/en/services/local-seo).

## FAQ

### Why did organic traffic drop?

Common causes: algorithm updates, lost links, indexation bugs, cannibalization, or seasonality. Audit before rewriting everything.
