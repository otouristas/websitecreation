The property market runs online: "apartment for sale [area]", "real estate agent [neighborhood]", "holiday home [island]". **Real estate SEO** has a dual goal: bring **buyers** to your listings and - the more valuable one - bring **sellers** looking for an agent to list with.

## What Makes Real Estate SEO Different

Big portals dominate generic listing searches. An agency doesn't fight them head-on - it wins in two areas the portals don't cover:

1. **Local expertise**: "estate agent + area" and "property valuation + area" are seller searches - the client who gives you the listing.
2. **Area content**: neighborhood guides, prices by area, processes (residency schemes, taxes, transfers) - where you build the authority portals lack.

## Strategy in 5 Steps

### 1. Area pages, not just listings

An "Apartments in [area]" page with market analysis, prices, and current listings ranks for both buyers and sellers. Listings change - the area page stays and grows stronger.

### 2. Target sellers

"How much is my home worth", "what commission does an agent charge", "how to sell a property without stress". Content answering these brings listings - the root of agency income.

### 3. Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Your profile with seller and buyer reviews decides "estate agent near me" searches. See [local SEO](/en/services/local-seo).

### 4. International audience where it pays

For tourist areas and residency-scheme buyers, English (and often more) pages with correct hreflang open a market local portals don't touch. See [real-estate solutions](/en/solutions/real-estate).

### 5. Schema for listings

RealEstateListing structured data helps Google understand price, size, and location - and helps AI models surface your listings for relevant questions.

## FAQ

### I already post to portals. What does SEO add?

Portals rent you an audience - your site captures one. Every listing that comes through your own page is a client with no per-listing cost, and no ten competitors beside you on the same screen.

### How fast will I see results?

Seller searches in specific areas have relatively low competition - top rankings in 2-4 months are realistic. Area content compounds for years.

### Do I need a new site?

If your current site can't support area pages and fast listing search on mobile, yes. See [real-estate website creation](/en/solutions/real-estate/website-creation).
