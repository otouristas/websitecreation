**SEO for hotels** is no longer just “rank for hotel + destination.” Guests compare OTAs, Google Hotels, Maps and AI trip planners before they book. The winners own a fast site, clear room intent pages, and proof that earns citations.

We build and optimize hospitality sites across Greece — see [Hotels Santorini](/en/work/hotels-santorini), [Onoma Hotel](/en/work/onoma-hotel), [Kipos Hotel](/en/work/kipos-hotel) and the full [hotel solutions hub](/en/solutions/hotels).

## The hotel SEO stack that converts

1. **Room-type & amenity pages** — unique titles/H1s for “suite with pool”, “family room”, not one generic homepage.
2. **LodgingBusiness / Hotel schema** — rooms, check-in, geo, offers.
3. **Direct booking CTAs** — above the fold on mobile; WhatsApp + form.
4. **Local + destination SEO** — island/city landing pages for airports, beaches, villages.
5. **GEO / AEO** — FAQ answers AI planners can cite when users ask “best hotel in …”.

## Common mistakes

- Thin template pages that match every competitor
- Image-heavy galleries without compression or alt text
- Relying only on Booking.com branding (you train Google that OTAs own the query)
- No Greek/English hreflang when you sell to both markets
