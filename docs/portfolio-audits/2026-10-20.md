# Client portfolio review · 20 October 2026

All 42 client projects were reviewed individually using their public homepage and, where available, one or two relevant about/service pages. The browser capture pass produced 37 refreshed site images. Security checks or transient access failures are recorded as unverified; they are not evidence that a business has closed. Existing images are retained for those records. Way to Crete is retained as an archived project. Project Shadow is active and is no longer labelled offline.

Each English and Greek brief now identifies the actual business, its offer, the visitor's need, the website journey and relevant search topics. These observations do not claim measured SEO growth, published schema, performance scores or conversion uplift. Historical service tags are retained from the project records. Client reviews remain hidden.

Key corrections: Navos is a business strategy advisor, Project Shadow a WooCommerce product, Ktima Orion an event venue, Rethemnos a local publication, Nutrinest a clinical dietitian, Helicro a Belgian transport service and Villa Olivia Clara a villa on Sifnos. Shared Naxos Auto Rent and AGGELOS brand relationships are preserved. The homepage now showcases travel, hospitality, AI software and events together.

## Search-performance source data

Four charts use actual daily Search Console records for 20 September–17 October 2026. There are 28 complete, unique dates per site; chart totals are computed from those rows and the published CSV is identical. ONOMA Hotel: 1,563 clicks / 19,776 impressions. Mykonos Luxury: 638 / 49,904. MastoRush: 319 / 4,720. Helicro: 182 / 1,273.

Only ONOMA has a complete preceding 28-day comparison in the supplied dataset (1,545 clicks). Partial prior windows for the other sites are not compared or extrapolated. These are observed performance series, not a causal before/after experiment or an attribution of gains to our work. No queries, visitor identities, credentials or customer account IDs are included.

Run `node scripts/check-client-results.mjs` to verify coverage, counts and source exports. Reviews and growth claims were not invented.
