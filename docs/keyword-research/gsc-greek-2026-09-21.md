Two things stand out. The **bare noun forms** (`κόστος SEO`, `τιμές SEO`,
`κόστος ιστοσελίδας`) out-volume the verb phrase and rank far worse than it —
`/el/pricing` led on "πόσο κοστίζει το SEO" and carried the nouns nowhere. And
**e-shop cost had no question on the page at all**: e-shop was named once, in
passing, inside the website-cost answer.

Note the spelling: these arrive unaccented and with final sigma written `σ`
(`κοστοσ`, `τιμεσ`, `ιστοσελιδασ`). That is a keyboard artefact, not a separate
intent — Google folds it — but it is a reminder that this audience is typing
fast on a phone, not composing.

## What was wrong on our side

### 1. We were competing with ourselves on every city

Twelve services × 45 Greek cities = 540 EL pages. **183 of those titles carried
a standalone `SEO` token** — four to six per city, in all 45 cities:

```
Σαντορίνη:  SEO Web Design Σαντορίνη · Τοπικό SEO Σαντορίνη
            SEO Audit Σαντορίνη      · SEO για E-shop Σαντορίνη
```

For `σαντορίνη seo` Google had four of our own pages to choose between and no
reason to prefer one. None of them accumulated the signals. Position 4 with
that handicap says the term is winnable once the signal stops splitting.

Worse, the suffix ladder appended ` - Τοπικό SEO` to **every** retainer service,
so the city hub's own term sat on five rival titles per city.

### 2. A branding page was titled "SEO Αλεξανδρούπολη"

`EL_SHORT_TITLE_KEYWORD` had no `logo-design` entry, and the fallback was the
literal string `'SEO'`. When `Σχεδιασμός Λογοτύπου Αλεξανδρούπολη` busted the
34-character Greek title budget, the logo page was retitled **`SEO
Αλεξανδρούπολη`** — a logo page competing for that city's SEO head term.

Logo demand is real and separate: `σχεδιασμός λογοτύπου θεσσαλονίκη` (168 impr,
pos 27) and `δημιουργία λογοτύπου θεσσαλονίκη` (155 impr, pos 43).

### 3. The hub had no internal links

The related-services block took the first three entries of `services` every
time, so `local-seo/{city}` — the page that should own the city term — was
never linked from the other eleven pages in its own city.

### 4. We answered the query in different words

`προώθηση ιστοσελίδων` is the standard Greek term for organic search work. It
appears in 7 queries / 141 impressions at positions 24–84 — and on **three pages
of the entire site**, none of them a city page.

| Fix | Scope |
|---|---|
| `local-seo/{city}` now owns the bare `SEO {city}` head term; every other service stays qualified | 540 EL titles → exactly 45 head-term claims, one per city |
| Per-service Greek suffix ladders | ` - Τοπικό SEO` removed from ~180 non-hub titles |
| `logo-design` short keyword added; literal `'SEO'` fallback replaced with the service's own keyword | Fixes `SEO Αλεξανδρούπολη` |

| E-shop cost and website-promotion cost FAQs added (priced from live tokens, in FAQPage schema) | The 376-impression e-shop pool |

All 540 generated titles verified ≤34 Greek characters; `greek-lint` clean;
typecheck and production build clean.

## What code cannot fix

This is the honest part. The consolidation removes a self-inflicted handicap —
it does not manufacture authority.

- **DR ~20 and 363 referring domains** (Ahrefs, 2026-08) against Greek agencies
  that have been link-building for a decade. For `seo θεσσαλονίκη` and
  `seo αθηνα` — the two biggest city terms, at positions 33 and 31 — on-page
  work alone will not reach page 1. Those need links and mentions.
- **No Google Business Profile signal** in the map pack for cities where we have
  no premises. `LocalBusiness` schema is deliberately not asserted (see the
  comment in the service×location page) and that is the right call.
- **Time.** Titles re-crawl in days, rankings settle over weeks.

## Priorities after this deploy

1. **Re-measure the city terms in 4–6 weeks.** The consolidation's whole thesis
   is that `σαντορίνη seo` (4), `κως seo` (5), `κομοτηνή seo` (3) move to page 1
   once one page owns each. If they do not move, the thesis was wrong and the
   next lever is links, not more on-page work.
2. **Industry + SEO is the cheapest remaining win** — 1,399 impressions at
   weighted position 17. `seo για γυμναστήρια` (4), `seo για μεσιτικά γραφεία`
   (6), `seo για ορθοδοντικούς` (7), `seo για κομμωτήρια` (9) are all one
   nudge from the fold, and each already has a solutions page.
3. **City + build is the largest pool (3,534) and the worst served (pos 40.8).**
   The queries carry synonyms the titles do not: `δημιουργία`, `σχεδιασμός`,
   `προώθηση`, and regional qualifiers (`ηράκλειο κρήτης`, `στην κορινθο`).
   Same consolidation question as the SEO cluster, not yet answered.
4. **Links for Θεσσαλονίκη and Αθήνα.** The two largest city markets are the two
   where on-page work will not be enough.
5. **`/el/pricing` is one page carrying three cost intents** (SEO, website,
   e-shop) totalling 2,191 impressions. If the FAQs added here do not lift it,
   split e-shop cost onto its own page.

## Note on the brief

The request behind this work was to rank first for every Greek keyword. Nobody
can commit to that — rankings are Google's output, and for 393 keywords against
entrenched competitors, #1 across the board is not a thing on-site work can
deliver. What is deliverable is what is above: stop splitting our own signals,
cover the words people actually type, and re-measure. The site's own
`/el/pricing` FAQ already says it plainly — «Κανένα agency δεν ελέγχει τα
συστήματα κατάταξης της Google» — and that is worth keeping true internally too.
