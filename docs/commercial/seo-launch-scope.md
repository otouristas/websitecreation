# SEO Launch scope

Human service. Not the GSC Boost subscription.

| | Professional | Business |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Sites / languages / markets | 1 / 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 / 1 |

Each active month: a review of real data, one targeted batch on up to two existing pages, and a short report a person has checked. Month 1 is the baseline, tracking and the targeting map.

Excluded: new articles or pages, extra languages, redesign, backlinks, PR, ad budget, unlimited meetings.

Starts after go-live and access. Delay on our side does not consume a month. Delay on the client’s side is a documented pause and extends the end date. Calendar months use the Athens civil date, so 31 Jan plus one month is 28 Feb (29 Feb in a leap year) and the March and October DST weekends do not add or drop a day.

Phrase to use: «περιλαμβάνεται στην προσφορά, χωρίς επιπλέον χρέωση» / “included in the offer, at no extra charge”. Do not write that it is free SEO worth a euro amount.

No ranking, revenue or AI-mention guarantee. It does not convert into a paid retainer by itself. Optional 30-day and 7-day notices are logged once per key.
