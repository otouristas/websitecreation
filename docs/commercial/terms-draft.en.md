# Build and offer terms — draft

**Status: requires legal review.** This text is not the binding contract. It does not name a legal entity, tax number, corporate form or jurisdiction. The supplier’s details are those published on the site and repeated on the quote. The binding version is the one issued with the accepted quote, with a terms version id.

## 1. Scope

The quote names pages, language, integrations and deliverables. Starter: up to 5 pages, 1 language, a form, technical SEO, one training session of up to 60 minutes. Professional: up to 12 pages, keyword mapping, structured data. Business: up to 25 pages. E-commerce, migration and booking are included only when the quote names them. A booking link or widget, a provider integration and a custom app are separate lines. Second-language structure and translation are separate lines.

## 2. Revisions

Two consolidated revision rounds. Work outside the agreed scope becomes a change order and starts after written approval.

## 3. Materials and approvals

The client supplies copy, images and access that do not infringe third-party rights. Delay in materials or approval is recorded and moves the schedule. It does not consume SEO Launch months that have not started.

## 4. Initial defect support

For 30 days after delivery we fix defects in our own implementation, at no charge. New requests, content and scope changes are not defects.

## 5. Extra work

€80/hour before VAT. Minimum 30 minutes (€40), then 15-minute steps. Overage only with a new approval.

## 6. Support hours

Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00, Europe/Athens. The official queue is email and the portal. An automatic acknowledgement is not a response. First response: no plan 2 business days, Care and Care Plus 1 business day, Partner 4 business hours. These are not resolution times and the service is not 24/7.

## 7. Build offer and SEO Launch

The 30% discount applies only to the base build cost. Professional: SEO Launch for 3 months (6 hours total). Business: SEO Launch for 6 months (12 hours total). Up to 2 hours of human work per active month, for 1 website, 1 language and 1 market. Included in the offer, at no extra charge. It does not renew automatically and it does not convert into monthly SEO. Hosting, domains, licences, extra pages, integrations, booking, maintenance, paid SEO and software are not discounted unless the quote names them. We do not guarantee rankings, revenue or AI mentions.

## 8. External costs

Third-party costs are invoiced separately and agreed beforehand.

## 9. Intellectual property and access

After payment, the client receives the agreed deliverables and the project’s access. Tools, third-party libraries and pre-existing material stay under their own licences. We do not remove the discount retroactively from a quote that has been accepted.

## 10. Cancellation

Cancellation closes with an itemised settlement of work and costs already incurred. There is no blanket “no refunds” clause. Amounts for work that was not delivered are dealt with in that settlement.

## 11. Monthly plans

Care, Care Plus, Partner and paid monthly SEO have no hidden minimum term beyond what that specific quote states. SEO Launch is not a paid monthly plan. Plan hours do not roll over, except a documented delay on our side.

## 12. Consumer and business

If the client is a consumer, statutory withdrawal rights are not cut down by this draft. If work starts during a withdrawal window, that is asked for explicitly and recorded. Business-to-business deals are not presented as consumer deals. The classification is confirmed on the quote, not by this draft.

## 13. Acceptance

Acceptance stores an immutable copy of price, scope, campaign and terms version, and gives the client a copy.
