## Recommended Meta campaign structure

### Campaign

- Objective: Leads
- Conversion location: Website first; test an Instant Form as a second ad set.
- Optimisation event: completed quote request.
- Attribution: 7-day click / 1-day view as an initial Meta default.

### Ad set 1 — Prospecting

- Audience: tourism and hospitality business owners/operators.
- Initial regions: Greece, Cyprus, Italy, Spain, Portugal and Croatia; narrow or expand after approval.
- Age: 25–60.
- Placements: Advantage+ placements, with mobile-first creative.
- Creative test: Ad A vs. Ad B.

### Ad set 2 — Retargeting

- Website visitors: 30 and 90 days.
- Facebook and Instagram engagers: 365 days.
- Exclude completed quote leads.
- Use proof-led Ad A first.

### Instant Form fields

- Full name
- Work email
- Phone or WhatsApp
- Business name
- Current website
- Business type: hotel / villa / tours / car rental / other
- Main goal: new website / redesign / SEO / GEO-AEO
- Optional project budget range

### Measurement

- Primary KPI: qualified lead volume and cost per qualified lead.
- Secondary: landing-page views, form completion rate, CTR and booked discovery calls.
- Review cadence: after the first 1,500–3,000 impressions per creative, then twice weekly.
- Do not scale solely on cheap leads; evaluate business type, project fit and response quality.

## Launch dependencies

1. Confirm target countries and daily budget.
2. Confirm whether to use website leads, an Instant Form, or both.
3. Confirm the organic posting schedule.
4. Verify Meta Pixel/Conversions API events on the quote form before launch.
5. Publish only after reviewing the final campaign summary and projected spend.
