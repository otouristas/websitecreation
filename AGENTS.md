# Greek copy and product identity

- The assistant is **Aster AI**, always in full, including hero copy, buttons,
  demonstrations, metadata, accessibility labels, platform pages and screenshots.
  The app is **AnotherSEOGuru App**. Other companies' products retain their own names.
- Write Greek as original, natural Greek. Do not translate English sentence structure
  or metaphors literally. Use professional, polite plural and explain the practical
  benefit to a reader who does not know SEO.
- Follow [the Greek style guide](docs/greek-style-guide.md). Read each complete screen
  and rewrite unnatural copy; passing a glossary check does not establish good Greek.
- Review Greek and English together when changing product copy. Keep marketing names
  consistent with the web, iOS, Android, WordPress and browser-extension experience.
- Do not invent testimonials, results, customer endorsements, or availability.
  Preserve sources and date ranges for actual metrics; label estimates as estimates.
- The reviewed `src/data/portfolio-research.ts` records and the dated portfolio audit
  are the current source for client descriptions. Old scrape correction files are
  historical context. Do not regenerate SEO or implementation claims from them.
  Run `node scripts/validate-portfolio-case-studies.mjs` after portfolio edits.
